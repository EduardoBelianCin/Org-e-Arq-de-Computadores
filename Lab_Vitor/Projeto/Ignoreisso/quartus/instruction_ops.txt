Switch-controlled operation map (current pl_control/pl_alu_ctrl support):
SW[10:5] = A, SW[16:11] = B, SW[4:0] = operation; SW[17] unused.
A and B are interpreted as signed 6-bit values for signed arithmetic/comparisons;
bitwise/unsigned operations use their zero-extended six-bit values.

00: ADD
01: SUB
02: AND
03: OR
04: XOR
05: SLT
06: SLTU
07: SLL
08: SRL
09: SRA
10: ADDI
11: ANDI
12: ORI
13: XORI
14: SLTI
15: SLTIU
16: SLLI
17: SRLI
18: SRAI
19: LUI
20: AUIPC
21: BEQ
22: LW
23: SW
Immediate operation constants: ADDI +1, ANDI &0x1f, ORI |1, XORI ^1,
SLTI/SLTIU compare against 1, shifts by 1. LUI uses immediate 1; AUIPC uses 0.
LW/SW use word index B (byte address B*4); SW stores A and displays A.
BEQ displays 1 when A==B, else 0.
